# Tests

## Test 1 - Cashier completes a cash POS sale and stock updates [sanity]
Viewport: desktop
Covers: authentication, POS cart workflow, sale creation, stock deduction, invoice/receipt generation, sales history persistence
Description: Verifies the core revenue workflow - a sale actually decrements inventory and is recorded, not just simulated in the UI.

Steps:
1. Sign in with username "peter" and PIN "1234"
2. Go to Point of Sale and note the stock count shown on the "Tecno Spark 20" product card
3. Tap the "Tecno Spark 20" product card once to add it to the cart, choose Cash as the payment method, enter an Amount Tendered of 20000, and tap Complete Sale
4. In the receipt modal, confirm the invoice number and total are shown, then close it
5. Go to Products & Inventory and find "Tecno Spark 20"
6. Go to Sales & Invoices

Expected: The stock count for "Tecno Spark 20" in Products & Inventory is exactly one less than the value noted in step 2, and Sales & Invoices shows a new invoice at the top with payment method "Cash" and status "Completed".

## Test 2 - Add a new product and edit it, changes persist after reload
Viewport: desktop
Covers: product CRUD create/update, category assignment, database persistence after reload
Description: Verifies product management is a real, durable workflow rather than a mock form.

Steps:
1. Sign in with username "admin" and PIN "1234"
2. Go to Products & Inventory and click "Add Product"
3. Fill in Product Name "Test Bluetooth Speaker", SKU "SKU-TEST-001", Selling Price 3500, Current Stock 10, then click "Add Product" to save
4. Search for "Test Bluetooth Speaker" in the product list, click the edit (pencil) icon, change the Selling Price to 3999, and save
5. Refresh the page and search for "Test Bluetooth Speaker" again

Expected: "Test Bluetooth Speaker" is visible in the product list with a selling price of KES 3,999 after the refresh.

Description: Verifies the M-Pesa payment flow is backend-driven - the sale only finalizes after the transaction is confirmed as successful, not immediately on button click.

Steps:
1. Sign in with username "jane" and PIN "1234"
2. Go to Point of Sale, add "Wireless Earbuds Pro" to the cart
3. Select M-Pesa as the payment method, enter phone number "0711222333", and tap Complete Sale

Expected: A receipt modal appears showing payment method "MPESA" and the sale total, confirming the payment was verified and the sale completed automatically without further user action.
